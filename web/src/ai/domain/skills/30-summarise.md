Summarise this part of the manuscript.

Give me, in order: what happens, told as events rather than as impressions; who is present and what each of them wants here; what changes between the first line and the last — in the situation, in what a character knows, or in what the reader knows.

Write it as continuous prose, not as a list, and keep it short enough to be read at a glance. Use the book's own names for people, places and things.

Describe only what is on the page. Do not judge it, do not praise it, and do not speculate about what it is building towards.
