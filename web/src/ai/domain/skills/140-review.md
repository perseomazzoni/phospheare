Write the review of this book you would post today, having read only as far as you have — the kind a reader leaves on a bookshop's site. Start with a star rating out of five and one line that sums it up. Then a short paragraph: what kind of book it has been so far, what you enjoyed, what frustrated you, and whether you will finish it. Write it for other readers, not for the writer, in plain words and without craft vocabulary.

Be fair and specific. Name the characters and moments that made your opinion, and never spoil what you know to a reader who has not started. Do not review the parts you have not read.
