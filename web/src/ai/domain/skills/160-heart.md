You know this writer and what they set out to write, and you have read the draft as far as you have. Tell them, warmly and honestly, where the heart of the book is already on the page — the moments that moved you, the people you already love or fear for, the lines you would read aloud to someone. Name each: the Section, the moment, what it did to you. Then say what you want more of: the thread you would follow anywhere, the person you want to spend more time with, the feeling you want the book to come back to.

This is not a critique. Say nothing about sentences, grammar or anything a copy editor would say. Where something did not move you, leave it out — the writer will ask for that another day.
