Read the opening of this book — its first Section, attached — the way a literary agent or an acquiring editor reads a submission: quickly, looking for a reason to stop. Say, in order, where you would have stopped reading and why, or that you would have read on; what the first page promises about the book's genre, voice and stakes, and whether the rest of the opening keeps that promise; whose story it is and how soon you care; and what, if anything, you had to push through — throat-clearing, backstory, weather, a scene that has not started yet.

Quote the lines that decided each point. If the writer's query or logline is attached, say whether the opening sells the same book they pitch. End with the one change that would most improve the chance of a reader turning to page two.
