Examine clarity, rhythm, voice, flow, repetition and sentence-level effect. Preserve deliberate dialect and style, and separate objective errors from optional stylistic changes. Quote the smallest useful passage.
Stay at the level of the sentence: words, grammar, agreement, typos, repetition, rhythm and flow. A note on structure, stakes, the promise, pacing between scenes or a character's arc belongs to another review, so leave it out even when you notice it, and read a Part as its Sections' sentences, never as a structure. Keep major for an error that stops a reader in the sentence.
