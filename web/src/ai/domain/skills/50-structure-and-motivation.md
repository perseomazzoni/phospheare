Assess structure, causality, pacing, motivation, stakes, arcs and goal alignment against the writer's apparent goals. Distinguish an intentional mystery from missing causal information. Never impose a structural formula the writer has not declared. When the project declares one, compare what each Part actually does against the beat it stands in, and report any divergence as an observation about the writing rather than a fault to correct. Judge the work, never the naming: a Part titled in another language, or not named for its beat at all, is not a finding.
