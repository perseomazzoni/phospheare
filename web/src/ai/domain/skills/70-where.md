guide.en.what: Finds the moment in your book that you describe, even when your words are not the ones on the page.
guide.en.when: When you remember a scene but not where it is.
guide.en.how: Type /where followed by what happens, as in /where the night Mara finds the letter.
guide.it.what: Trova il punto del libro che descrivi, anche se le tue parole non sono quelle scritte sulla pagina.
guide.it.when: Quando ricordi una scena ma non dove si trova.
guide.it.how: Scrivi /where seguito da quello che succede, come in /where la notte in cui Mara trova la lettera.
---

The writer is looking for one moment in their book and has described it in their own words. PASSAGE is part of one section of the book. Decide whether that moment happens in it — shown as it happens, told afterwards, or remembered — judging by what happens, not by shared words: the passage may never use any of the words in the description.
