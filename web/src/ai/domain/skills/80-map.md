The writer wants to see how their manuscript, or one Part of it, sits on a structure: the one they declared for the project, or one they chose for this map. Each ROW is one Part or Section of the book, told through summaries of what happens in it. Judge what it DOES for the story — the work it carries, from what happens — never its title or its wording, and never against a formula the writer did not choose. A summary may be out of date or wrong; judge the book it describes, and say so plainly when a row is too thin to judge.
