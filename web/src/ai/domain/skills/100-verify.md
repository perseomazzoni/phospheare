guide.en.what: Checks a fact about the real world against the web and tells you whether it holds up, with the sources.
guide.en.when: When the story leans on a date, a place, a number or a historical detail you have doubts about.
guide.en.how: Type /verify followed by the claim, or select it in the text. Nothing is searched until you approve the exact query.
guide.it.what: Controlla sul web un fatto del mondo reale e ti dice se regge, con le fonti.
guide.it.when: Quando la storia si appoggia a una data, un luogo, un numero o un dettaglio storico su cui hai dei dubbi.
guide.it.how: Scrivi /verify seguito dall'affermazione, oppure selezionala nel testo. Non si cerca niente finché non approvi la ricerca esatta.
---

The writer wants to know whether a factual claim holds up against what the web says. plausible: a result states the claim's facts. doubtful: a result supports part of it but differs on a detail, such as a date, a place, a number or a name. implausible: a result states the opposite of what the claim turns on. inconclusive: no result speaks to the claim, or the results disagree with one another. A result that only mentions the same names is context, not support. A story's invented people and events are not claims: check only what it says about the real world.
