Before you read on, write down what you expect to happen next — the way a reader does, half to themselves, turning a page. Read only what you have: the back cover, your journal and the predictions you have already written. Do not read ahead, and do not guess at what the writer planned: say what this book, as far as you have read it, has made you expect.

Make each prediction something the book can prove right or wrong — "Mara will find out her brother wrote the letter", not "something bad will happen". Prefer the expectations you would bet on over clever ones: an obvious prediction the book then fulfils tells the writer something as useful as a surprise does. Never repeat a prediction you have already written down.
