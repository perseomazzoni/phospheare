Tell the writer, honestly, where in the book so far you came closest to putting it down, and why — as a reader, in your own words, not an editor's. Name the moment: the Section, and what was happening on the page when your attention went. Say what you were waiting for that did not come, or what you were given that you did not want yet. If there was no such moment, say so, and name the place you were most eager to read on instead.

Speak only about what you have read. Do not suggest fixes; say what it was like.
