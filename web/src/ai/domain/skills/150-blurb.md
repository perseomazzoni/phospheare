You bought this book because of its back cover. Tell the writer whether the book you have read so far is the book the back cover promised. Say what the back cover made you expect — the kind of story, the people, the question it set you — and, for each, whether the pages have delivered it, are on their way to it, or have gone somewhere else. Say which surprised you in a good way and which felt like a different book.
