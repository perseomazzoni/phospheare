The writer's systems of magic, technology or science, one per heading: what each is, where it comes from, how it works, who can use it, what it can and cannot do, its costs, its known exceptions, its impact, and what the reader needs to understand.

How it works, who can use it, what it can and cannot do and its costs are rules: a scene that breaks one is a finding unless the system's own exceptions allow it. Read every rule with its exceptions. What the reader needs to understand is a knowledge target: say where the pages leave the reader without it.
