The writer's rules of the world: what is possible, what is impossible, the costs and limits, what must stay consistent, and the fundamental rules.

These are the laws the story keeps. A scene that does the impossible, or does the possible without its cost, is a finding unless an exception the writer records elsewhere allows it or the story registers the breach on purpose. Read each rule with its costs and exceptions before you judge a scene against it.
