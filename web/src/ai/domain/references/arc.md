The writer's plan for how one person changes over the book: where they start, what sets them moving, the turning point, the crisis, what changes and where they end up. The arc belongs to the character whose name it carries.

Everything on this page is intended story. It may be written already, partly written or still to come: never report a planned moment as missing from pages that are not written yet, and never treat a plan as proof of what the prose does. Where the draft and the plan part ways, say where, quoting both, and leave the choice to the writer. Use the arc to judge whether a scene moves the person along it, or against it on purpose.
