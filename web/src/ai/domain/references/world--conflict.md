The writer's notes on the world's conflicts and tensions: the situation, who is involved, how it began, what could change it, how it connects to the novel, and the struggles for power beneath it.

How a conflict began and who holds power are established facts. The rest is material and pressure to draw on, and what could change or erupt is open. Use the page to judge whether scenes carry the tension the writer describes, never to demand that every tension appear.
