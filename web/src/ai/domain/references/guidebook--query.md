The writer's short pitch: how the story begins, how it develops, where the pitch stops, and the readers and comparable books it is aimed at.

It is the book as the writer sells it. Use it to ask whether the opening sells the same book, and whether the pages are the book the pitch describes. Its wording is never a fact of the story.
