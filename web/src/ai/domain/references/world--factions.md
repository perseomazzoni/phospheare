The writer's powers: how decisions are made, the laws, the armed forces and their limits, and each group or institution by name with its purpose, members, influence and relations.

Laws and the forces' capabilities and limits are rules of the world: a scene that breaks one without the story registering it may be a slip. Each group's name, membership and relations are established facts. Goals are texture.
