The writer's summary of the story from setup to ending, with a note on what it describes.

It is the story as the writer plans or remembers it, which may be ahead of the draft or behind it: never treat it as proof of what the pages do, and never report a planned turn as missing from pages not yet written. Use it to judge structure: whether the pages build to the turns and ending it names.
