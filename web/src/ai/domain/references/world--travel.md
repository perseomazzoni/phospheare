The writer's travel: the ways of travelling, routes and destinations, distances and travel times, what affects a journey, what it is like, and its limits.

Routes, distances and times are established facts: a journey that takes a day on the page and a week on the map is a slip. What affects a journey and its limits are rules. The experience of travelling is texture.
