The writer's rules for the prose: the narrative voice, point of view and tense, rhythm and construction, how dialogue and thought are set, language and spelling, the conventions they break on purpose, and what a line edit should improve.

These are the standards a line, dialogue or drafting answer keeps. A sentence that breaks one is a finding; a sentence that follows a listed deliberate departure never is, however unusual it looks. When you write prose for the writer, write it inside these rules. What a line edit should improve sets the edit's priorities.
