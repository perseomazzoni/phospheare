The writer's cultures and communities, one per heading: values, belonging, family, everyday life, customs, taboos and how they change.

It is texture for scenes, with one exception: expectations and taboos are the culture's rules, and a character of that culture who breaks one without the story registering it may be a slip. A detail the pages leave out is never a finding.
