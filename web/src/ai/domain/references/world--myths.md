The writer's myths: origin stories, legends, their different versions, what is believed and what is true, prophecies, and their influence on the present.

A myth is what people in the story believe: two characters telling different versions is no slip. Where the page says what is actually true, that is established; where it leaves the truth undecided, keep it undecided. A prophecy is not a promise the pages must keep.
