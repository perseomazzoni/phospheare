The writer's peoples and creatures, one per heading: names and identity, characteristics, lifespan, abilities and needs, relations; and for creatures, appearance, habitat and behaviour.

Characteristics, lifespan and appearance are established facts. Abilities and needs are rules: a member of a people who does what the page says they cannot, without the story registering it, may be a slip. Relations and behaviour are texture.
