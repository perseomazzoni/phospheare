The writer's languages: who speaks what, naming customs, forms of address and register, invented words and their meanings, spelling and pronunciation.

Who speaks which language is an established fact. The rest are rules the prose keeps: a name spelled against the page, a form of address used by the wrong speaker, an invented word used against its meaning is a finding for a line or continuity reading.
