The writer's research notebook: questions still to investigate, sources, what is real and what is invented, other perspectives, and corrections still to carry into the draft.

The line between what is real and what is invented is a rule: never report an invention the writer declares as a factual error. The questions and other perspectives are open. The corrections are already on the writer's list: when the pages still carry one, say it is the known one. Sources are material to draw on, not facts of the story.
