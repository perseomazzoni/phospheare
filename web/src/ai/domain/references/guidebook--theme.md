The writer's themes: the central theme, recurring motifs and images, the questions the book asks, where the themes surface, the answer the writer is exploring and who argues the other side.

It is intent, a goal to read against. Use it to say where the pages carry a theme, where a motif lapses, and where the other side is argued too weakly to be heard. Never report the pages for failing to state a theme outright.
