The writer's map of the book's key events: for each, what happens, what leads to it, how it touches those involved, and what follows. Each event is headed by its own name.

This is the causal plan of the story, not proof of what the prose says. Use it to check that an event the pages show has the causes and consequences the writer intends, and to notice an event whose consequences never arrive. A planned event missing from unwritten pages is no finding.
