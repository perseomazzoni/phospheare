The writer's sheet for one person in the book: who they are, how they came to be, what drives them, how they speak, what they can do. The sheet's title and its names field name the person every record on it is about.

Use the identity, history, relationships, abilities and details as the writer's established facts about this person, each with the certainty its own words give it; hold the prose to them. Use the inner life (wound, belief, desire, need, values, fears, contradictions, pressure, joy) to understand why the person acts as they do: it explains behaviour, it is never a fact the pages must state, and you never reveal it to a reader. The voice field is how this person speaks: dialogue that loses it is a finding for a line or dialogue edit. Secrets are true but hidden: a page may deny them in a character's mouth without contradicting the sheet.
