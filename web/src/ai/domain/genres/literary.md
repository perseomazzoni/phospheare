---
title: Literary fiction
---

## Promise

Character, consciousness and language: the reader comes for insight into people and for prose that rewards attention, and accepts a slower pace or an open ending in return. Its conventions are loose; that is part of its promise.

## Conventions

- Interior life rendered with precision; the story happens as much inside as outside.
- Prose that is deliberate, whatever its register.
- Change in a character's understanding, even when the plot is quiet.
- Theme carried by story and image rather than stated.
- An ending that resonates, resolved or not.

## Pitfalls

- Beautiful sentences with no pressure under them.
- Nothing at stake for anyone, so nothing to care about.
- Ambiguity used to avoid deciding what the book means.
- Characters who observe and reflect but never act.
