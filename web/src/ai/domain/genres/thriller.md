---
title: Thriller
---

## Promise

Danger, speed and stakes that keep rising: someone the reader cares about is up against a threat that could destroy them or much more, and the clock is running. The reader comes for tension, not for puzzles.

## Conventions

- A clear threat and a clear antagonist, or a force that acts like one.
- Stakes stated early and raised, personal as well as public.
- A deadline or clock the reader can feel.
- Reversals: the protagonist's plans fail and the situation worsens.
- A moment where the protagonist is at the antagonist's mercy.
- A climax the protagonist wins through their own action, at a price.

## Pitfalls

- A villain who is menacing only in description.
- Tension that resets to zero between set pieces.
- A protagonist rescued by luck or by someone else.
- Twists that contradict what the book has shown rather than reframe it.
