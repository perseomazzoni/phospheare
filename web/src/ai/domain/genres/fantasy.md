title: Fantasy
---

## Promise

A world with its own rules — magic, peoples, history — and a story that could only happen there. The reader comes for wonder and for a world that feels lived in, and expects its rules to hold.

## Conventions

- A world shown through what characters do and need, not through lectures.
- Magic or the impossible has rules, limits and costs, established before they decide the plot.
- A sense of wonder: at least some moments that exist to astonish.
- Conflict rooted in the world itself — its powers, peoples, history.
- A protagonist whose journey changes their place in that world.

## Pitfalls

- Exposition delivered in blocks before the reader cares.
- Magic that solves the problem the moment it is needed, with no cost.
- Borrowed furniture — generic kingdoms, prophecies, dark lords — never made particular.
- Invented words used faster than the reader can hold them.
