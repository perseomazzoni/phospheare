---
title: Contemporary fiction
---

## Promise

Life now, recognisably: relationships, work, families and choices the reader knows from their own world, told with enough truth that they see it freshly. The reader comes for recognition and for emotional honesty.

## Conventions

- A present-day world rendered with specific, current detail.
- Conflict drawn from ordinary life — family, love, work, identity — with real consequences.
- Characters who change, or fail to, in ways the reader believes.
- An emotional arc the reader can follow from beginning to end.
- An ending that settles the main question the book raised.

## Pitfalls

- Detail that is generic rather than observed.
- Problems that resolve without anyone having to change.
- A cast who all sound like the narrator.
- Topical issues standing in for character.
