---
title: Historical fiction
---

## Promise

A real past made present: a period recreated so the reader feels what it was to live then, and a story that could only happen in that time. The reader expects the history to be right, and the people to be of their time.

## Conventions

- The period established through daily detail — objects, work, speech, belief — not only through events.
- Real historical events and figures, when used, consistent with the record, or departures the book signals.
- Characters whose values and assumptions belong to their time.
- Stakes shaped by the period's pressures: law, class, religion, war, custom.
- A story that uses the history rather than decorating it.

## Pitfalls

- Modern attitudes worn by historical characters without friction.
- Research displayed rather than dramatised.
- Anachronisms in speech, objects or ideas.
- Real people doing things the record contradicts, unexplained.
