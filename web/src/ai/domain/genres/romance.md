---
title: Romance
---

## Promise

A love story at the centre, and an ending that leaves the couple together — happy for now or for good. The reader comes for the relationship: two people who are right for each other and a real reason they cannot yet have each other.

## Conventions

- The two leads meet early, and the meeting changes something for both.
- What keeps them apart comes from who they are, not only from circumstance.
- The relationship deepens in scenes the reader can feel, not in summary.
- A low point where the relationship seems lost, caused by the leads' own flaws or choices.
- A turn where one or both chooses the other at real cost.
- An ending that pays off the promise: together, and believably so.

## Pitfalls

- An obstacle a single honest conversation would end.
- One lead who exists only to serve the other's arc.
- Desire told rather than shown; chemistry asserted in narration.
- An ending that withholds the union the genre promised.
