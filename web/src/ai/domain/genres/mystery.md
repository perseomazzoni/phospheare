---
title: Mystery
---

## Promise

A puzzle and the pleasure of solving it: a crime or an unexplained event, someone who investigates, and an answer at the end that is surprising and fair — every piece the reader needed was on the page.

## Conventions

- The central question — usually a crime — is posed early and matters to someone.
- An investigator who reasons, whether professional or not.
- Clues the reader sees at the same time as the investigator, including the ones that matter most.
- Suspects with motive and opportunity, and at least one wrong answer the story takes seriously.
- A reveal that recasts earlier scenes, explained through evidence already shown.
- Justice, or its failure, addressed by the end.

## Pitfalls

- A solution that turns on information the reader never had.
- A culprit introduced too late to be suspected.
- Coincidence or confession doing the investigator's work.
- A middle where nothing new is learned for several chapters.
