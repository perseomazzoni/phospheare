---
title: Horror
---

## Promise

Fear, built slowly and paid off: dread before the terror, and a threat that feels as if it could reach the reader too. The reader comes to be frightened, and to feel the characters' vulnerability as their own.

## Conventions

- Dread established before the threat is shown in full.
- Characters the reader cares about, so what happens to them matters.
- A threat with rules, even if the characters do not know them.
- Isolation — physical, social or psychological — that cuts off escape.
- Escalation: each encounter is worse than the last.
- An ending that resolves or deliberately refuses to, and in either case leaves a mark.

## Pitfalls

- The monster shown too early and too clearly.
- Characters who act stupidly so the plot can happen.
- Gore standing in for fear.
- A final explanation that makes the threat smaller.
