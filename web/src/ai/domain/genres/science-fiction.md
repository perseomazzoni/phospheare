---
title: Science fiction
---

## Promise

An idea about what could be — a technology, a future, a changed society — followed through honestly to its human consequences. The reader comes for the idea and for what it does to people.

## Conventions

- A central speculative premise, clear by the end of the opening.
- The premise changes how people live, choose and relate; the story explores that change.
- Internal consistency: what the world allows once, it allows again.
- Science or technology plausible enough for the book's register, from hard to soft.
- Stakes that are human even when the scale is vast.

## Pitfalls

- The idea explained at length and dramatised little.
- A future that is only the present with gadgets.
- Characters who exist to demonstrate the premise.
- A premise abandoned once the plot gets going.
