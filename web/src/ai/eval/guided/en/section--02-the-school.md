Marta Neri taught Latin at a school above the harbour in Genoa, to boys who would rather have been out on the water. She had never studied medicine, and had never wanted to.

She lived in Genoa, in a flat she shared with her sister, and its windows looked at the cranes. In the evenings she corrected translations on the balcony while the ferries went out to Sardinia.

The boys knew her by her red scarf and the pile of grammars she carried under one arm.
