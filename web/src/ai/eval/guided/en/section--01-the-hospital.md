Marta Neri took the post at the San Giovanni hospital in Turin in the spring of 2011. She was a doctor now, the first in her family, and she wore the white coat as if someone might come and take it back.

Marta had her father's grey eyes and her mother's habit of saying nothing until she was sure. On her first night shift an old man asked her the colour of the river outside, and she said she had not looked.

She lived in Turin, in two rooms above a bakery on Via Nizza. Every morning the smell of bread came up through the floorboards before the alarm did.
