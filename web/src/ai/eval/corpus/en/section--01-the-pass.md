The bus left us at the foot of the pass on Friday 8 March 2019, just before dusk. Hollis Wren was waiting by the shelter in a red jacket and a black wool hat, stamping her boots against the cold.

"The hut is four miles up," Hollis said. "Two hours if the snow holds, three if it doesn't."

She was sixty-one years old and walked like someone half her age. She took my rucksack without asking and started up the track. I followed her lantern and counted my steps to keep from thinking about the drop.

At the hut she lit the stove, and the room filled with a yellow light. On the table lay a logbook bound in green cloth, open at a page from twenty years before.
