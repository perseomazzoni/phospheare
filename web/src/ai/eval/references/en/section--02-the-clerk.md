'Thirty,' Ilse told the harbour clerk when he asked her age, 'and I have run this ferry for ten of them.' He wrote it down without looking up.

That night she took the Varga ferry across the strait, lamps low, by the channel only her family knew. The blue hull of the ferry was black in the dark water.

Across the water the church bell struck three times, the signal that a boat had not come home.

In the morning Tom was waiting on the far side. His green eyes were sore from the wind.
