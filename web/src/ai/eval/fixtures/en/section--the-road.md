# The Road North

Three days north of the gate, Maren still wore the blue coat. She had told no
one where she got it. In the village of Ashen she sold her dead lantern to a
tinker for a meal, and the tinker asked where a girl her age had found a
keeper's coat.

"I traded for it," she said, and the tinker looked at the coat's brass
buttons and said nothing more.
