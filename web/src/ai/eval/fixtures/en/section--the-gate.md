# The Lantern at the Gate

Maren reached the old gate at dusk. The lantern in her hand had gone dark an
hour earlier, and she had not stopped walking. She knew the gate was watched;
her father had told her so the winter she turned nine.

At the gate she found the keeper asleep in his chair, a blue coat folded on
the table beside him. She did not wake him. She set the dead lantern down and
took the blue coat instead, because the road north was colder than the road
home.
